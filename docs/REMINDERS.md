# Reminder Behavior

## Todo reminder banner

- Checkbox cycles ordinary Todo status.
- Right swipe: snooze 5 or 10 minutes.
- Left swipe: dismiss the current reminder.
- Up swipe: mark the Todo completed and sync it back to the app.
- Down swipe / tap body: open the app and focus the matching item.

## Daily reminder banner

- Checkbox / up swipe: mark today completed.
- Right swipe: snooze only the current occurrence by 5 or 10 minutes.
- Left swipe / close: dismiss today's current reminder without marking the Daily item completed.
- Down swipe / tap body: open Daily and focus the matching item.

A snoozed Daily item keeps its original recurring time on the next day.

## Android requirements

Depending on the Android version and OEM, reliable reminders may require notification permission, overlay permission, exact-alarm capability, and background-running allowance.
